### Spike — the behavioural release this PR carries

`pod-kde-nested v2026.331.1638` is a behavioural fix: the nested Plasma session locked itself after
Plasma's 5-minute idle `Timeout`, turning the streamed desktop into a greeter nobody could type into.
R12 applies; the round was run in the leaf's own repo, on disposable pods, with no restarts (a restart
re-spawns the greeter with `--immediateLock` and confounds every reading):

```
control, no kscreenlockerrc:   uptime 0:35:42  ->  greeters=1, ScreenSaver.GetActive=true
                               uptime 1:07:37  ->  still locked
with the policy written into the image, same pod, no restart:
                               uptime 0:08:04  ->  greeters=0, ScreenSaver.GetActive=false
```

Two candidate policies were measured: `Autolock=false` + `Timeout=0` alone did **not** stop the lock;
`Lock=false` (`Daemon/Lock` is the master switch) does. The leaf's bed `check-kde-nested-locked`
re-proves it on a fresh disposable pod (`PASS`; `check-live` 7m16s, 42 steps: 40 passed, 0 failed,
2 skipped). THIS pod's beds prove the generation runs and the whole browser surface works on it; the
idle-window boundary itself is proved by that leaf bed, not re-run here for a second time.

### One environment failure, fixed before the gate

The first `check-kde-vnc-cdp-pod` attempt failed in `[image-build]` after 7m54s with
`rm: cannot remove '/tmp/aur-pkgs/kde-shell': No space left on device`. The host `/home` is **btrfs**
with `Device unallocated: 1.00MiB` and metadata at 25.33 of 26.00 GiB (97%): `df` reported ~140 GiB
"free" inside already-allocated data chunks, while a container build's metadata-heavy writes hit
`ENOSPC`. Root fix, and the reason the gate above is green: `charly clean --deep` removed 48 untagged
images store-wide (~30 GiB of data, 0.9 GiB of metadata), and the two beds were then run on the
reclaimed host. Nothing in this diff caused it and nothing in it changes.
